Store Pulse

Store owners never have time to read their own numbers. Every Monday at 8am this workflow reads the week's orders and expenses, works out what changed, asks AI to explain it in plain words, and emails the owner a report with charts.

  1. Every Monday, 8:00amScheduled trigger
  2. Reads 6,967 order lines and 96 expensesGoogle Sheets
  3. Cleans data and calculates totalsSales by day and channel, vs last period
  4. AI writes the insightsGemini
  5. Emails the owner a report with chartsGmail

Lead generation

Lead Gen Bot

Finding clients by hand takes days. You send one Telegram message with a niche and a city. The bot searches Google Maps, checks each business's website, scores every lead with AI, writes a personal opener, saves everything to Google Sheets and pings you on Telegram.

  1. You message the bot/leads real estate agency in Lekki, Lagos
  2. Searches Google MapsApify, 20 results per search
  3. Researches each websiteFlags businesses with no website
  4. AI qualifies and scores each leadGemini writes a personal opener
  5. Saves to Google Sheets, alerts youNo duplicates on re-runs

AI sales agent

Voice Sales Agent

Customers would rather send a voice note than type an order. This Telegram agent understands voice notes, text and payment screenshots. It answers questions from the product list, takes orders, confirms payments against open orders, and hands tricky chats to a human, so the store sells around the clock.

  1. Customer sends a voice note, text or receiptTelegram, with a typing indicator
  2. Voice notes transcribed, receipts readGemini audio and image analysis
  3. AI agent checks stock and pricesGemini, chat memory, calculator, product sheet
  4. Creates the order or confirms paymentGoogle Sheets, receipt matched to the open order
  5. Replies, and keeps the owner in the loopEscalates to a human, owner updates order status by command
